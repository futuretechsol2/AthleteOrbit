AthleteOrbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

AthleteOrbit (athleteorbit.org and the AthleteOrbit apps for iPhone and Android) is a product of FutureTech AI, Concord, North Carolina, USA ("FutureTech AI", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Questions: support@athleteorbit.org.

Who uses AthleteOrbit

WhoHow they use it
Parents and guardiansTrack their children's competitions, scores, schedules, costs and photos; message their gym; use the AI coach. Parent accounts are for adults 18 or older.
AthletesAthletes under 18 use AthleteOrbit through a parent's or guardian's account and never create their own. Athletes 18 or older may create their own account.
Gym and club ownersUpload their roster and season competition schedule, send announcements and answer parents' messages.
Meet organizersSell tickets to competitions, check tickets in at the door and send updates to ticket holders.

We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create an account. If you believe a child has, email us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: name, username, email address and password (passwords are held by our sign-in provider and never visible to us), and which device is signed in.
  • About athletes (entered by a parent, or by their gym on its roster): first name (gyms may enter full names), sport, level, team or program, girls/boys team, gym, coach notes, competitions, scores and results, skills and goals, practice and pain logs, costs, packing lists and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Photos you upload: competition photos, their date and size, and captions. Photos are private to your family unless you choose to share them with families of your gym at the same competition.
  • Gym features: rosters, season schedules, announcements, messages between a family and its gym, and requests to link an athlete.
  • Tickets: buyer name and email, tickets bought and check-in status. Organizers see this for their own events.
  • Print orders: the photos or Season Book you order, and the shipping name and address you enter at checkout.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, payment status and the last details Stripe shares (such as card brand), never your full card number. Organizers' bank details for payouts are held by Stripe, not by us.
  • Reminders and travel: reminder settings, an optional mobile number for text reminders, and an optional home city or airport.
  • AI features: questions you ask Coach G, and photos you choose to have read (score sheets, competition schedules).
  • Support: messages you send us, with the name, email and phone number you provide.
  • Usage: basic technical data such as browser type and pages used, and how you found us (for example a referral code), to keep the service working and improve it. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To run AthleteOrbit for you: show your information, build each athlete's schedule from their gym's season schedule, send reminders, deliver messages and announcements, sell and check tickets, and fulfill print orders.
  • To produce AI guidance and to read photos you choose. For competitions on your calendar, we search the web for the event's public details (host, website, venue, session times) using the event's name, date and location — not your personal information.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, and keep the service secure and reliable.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not use your data or your children's data to train AI models.

Who can see what

  • Your family's data is visible only to your account.
  • Your gym sees the athletes you link to its roster, the messages you send it and your account name and email. Other families searching a roster see only an athlete's first name, last initial, level and gym.
  • Shared photos are visible only to families of the same gym whose athletes are at the same competition. You can stop sharing or delete a photo at any time.
  • Meet organizers see the names, emails and check-in status of people who bought tickets to their events.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any record, photo or message you created.
  • Download your data in More › Download my data.
  • Delete your account in More › Delete account. This removes your family's data and photos and cancels your subscription. Gyms can ask us to delete their roster and schedule.
  • Turn off reminder emails from any reminder or in More › Reminders. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax, payment or legal reasons (such as ticket and order receipts), which we keep only as long as required.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules ensure each family can only reach its own records, and photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links. Only one device can be signed in to an account at a time. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also email account holders.
