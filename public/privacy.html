Who uses AthleteOrbit

AthleteOrbit accounts are for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Children do not create accounts. A parent may enter information about their own child, such as first name, gymnastics program and level, scores, meets and skill progress.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Information you enter: gymnast first names, program, level, club, scores, meets, costs, skill checklist, and notes.
  • AI features: questions you ask, score-sheet photos you choose, and still frames taken on your device from videos you choose. Full videos are never uploaded. Photos and frames are sent to our AI provider to produce a response and are not stored by AthleteOrbit. Written AI feedback you save is stored in your account.
  • Reminders and travel: your reminder choices, optional mobile number (only if you turn on text reminders) and home city or airport, used to send meet reminders and build travel links.
  • Family calendar: if you subscribe from another calendar app, that app reads your private calendar link. Anyone with that link can see your meets and family events, so keep it private.
  • Support: messages you send through Contact us, with the name, email and phone you provide.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status, not your full card number.

How we use it

Only to run the app for your family: show your data, generate AI guidance, process subscriptions, and provide support. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic (AI responses), Stripe (payments), SendGrid (email), and Twilio (text messages, only if you turn them on). Hotel and flight links open partner websites, which have their own privacy policies; some links may earn us a commission. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Your choices

Turn reminder emails off with one click in any reminder, or in More > Reminders & travel. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Edit or delete any record in the app. Delete your whole account in More > Delete account; this permanently removes your data and cancels your subscription. Or email us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure each family can only access its own records.
